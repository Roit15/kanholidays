Overview

Hotel Stay Private AC Transfers Daily Breakfast Sightseeing All Toll & Parking

Some islands can be seen in a few days, Europe deserves to be felt over many. Over these ten days, you move through every mood the island has to offer: vibrant northern beaches, dramatic southern cliffs, misty nature reserves, colonial-era city streets, and quiet stretches of untouched coastline, each adding another layer to the story.

It's a journey that doesn't rush from one highlight to the next, but instead lets you live inside the island's rhythm fully, day after unhurried day. With the KanHolidays 8 Nights 9 Days Europe Package, you get the complete Europe experience with comfortable stays, thoughtfully paced days, and a journey that leaves no side of this island jewel unexplored.
