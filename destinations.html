About Mauritius

Mauritius is an Indian Ocean island nation known for its beaches, lagoons and reefs. The mountainous interior includes Black River Gorges National Park, with rainforests, waterfalls, hiking trails and wildlife like the flying fox. Capital Port Louis has sites such as the Champs de Mars horse track, Eureka plantation house and 18th-century Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam Botanical Gardens.

Whether you're looking for a romantic honeymoon escape, a family adventure with water sports, or a luxury retreat, Mauritius offers an unparalleled tropical experience with a unique blend of Indian, African, French, and Chinese cultures.

Travel Tips

  • Currency: Mauritian Rupee (MUR). 1 INR ≈ 0.55 MUR. Cards widely accepted.
  • Language: English and French are official. Creole is widely spoken.
  • Plugs: Type C/G (Same as India/UK). 230V.
  • Transport: Renting a car is popular. Taxis are available but negotiate beforehand.
